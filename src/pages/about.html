I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Mechanical Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, working with Axel Krieger, Ph.D. since August 2026. I completed my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering and Materials Science at Duke University in 2026, advised by Patrick Codd, M.D. and Boyuan Chen, Ph.D. in the Brain Tool Lab.

I build full-stack robotic systems that connect sensing, inference, and control — across multisensory information streams from acoustics to imaging, and across optomechanical systems, embedded platforms, and learning-based models — to achieve reliable and scalable autonomy in high-stakes, unstructured settings. My vision is to turn rich sensory data into actionable intelligence, making precision robotic operation safer and more effective.

Before Duke, I co-led Futures+ across four countries and founded TEDxNITW. These experiences inform how I think about technology design for communities that need it most.

I served as Lab Manager at the Brain Tool Lab (2023–2026) and Instructor of Record for two semesters of an undergraduate experiential-learning course developing teleoperated surgical systems for rural clinics.

My master’s research at Duke was advised by Xiaoyue Ni; my bachelor’s research at NIT Warangal was advised by P. Bangaru Babu.
