I build robots that see, decide, and act — transforming multimodal sensing into precise autonomous action.

Postdoctoral fellow at Johns Hopkins University working with Axel Krieger on autonomous surgical robots for precision surgery. I received my PhD degree from Duke University, working with Patrick Codd and Boyuan Chen, across multimodal perception, surgical autonomy, and machine learning. My systems close the loop from raw sensory data to real-world intervention.
