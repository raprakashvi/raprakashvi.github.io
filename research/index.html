TumorMap: A Laser-Based Surgical Platform for 3D Tumor Mapping and Fully-Automated Tumor Resection

Multimodal surgical platform — leveraging optical coherence tomography (OCT), laser-induced fluorescence, RGB images, to generate 3D pathological maps with learning-based mapping, enabling autonomous resection through laser scalpel.
