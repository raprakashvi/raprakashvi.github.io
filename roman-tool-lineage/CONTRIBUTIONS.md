# Proposed contributions and claim gates

Phase II · 6 October 2026 · **exploratory results; proposed validated contributions**

The historical question is which knowledge must accompany a tool for people to reproduce, judge and use it, and how this affects persistence and change. C1 supplies the evidence; C2–C4 are empirical targets. AI-assisted retrieval is a disclosed method. Novelty and generalizability remain to be tested against prior literature and independently reviewed evidence. The first source contrasts are reported in [PILOT_FINDINGS.md](PILOT_FINDINGS.md).

## Candidate manuscript statements, conditional on the results

- **C1:** “We release a source-linked corpus that distinguishes objects, textual prescriptions, depictions and components, and quantifies its coverage and coding uncertainty.” Use only after independent validation and a released version.
- **C2:** “Across [matched source/actor settings], [specified operational knowledge] was retained/transformed/omitted, and independent evidence links this to [observed adoption or reproduction outcome].” Without that outcome evidence, report a documentary contrast and candidate boundary only.
- **C3:** “Within [named source series and tasks], the probability of an observed material change relative to a mechanical change is [estimate/bounds], with [replication result].” Do not extend a 1750–1950 study to all history.
- **C4:** “We distinguish [number] documented textual transfers, [number] supported design-adoption claims and [number] unresolved similarities.” Counts await adjudication; a textual transfer is not a tool transfer.

## What is outside the promised contribution

No claim of a first instrument worldwide, universal Roman ancestry, a single bronze-to-steel ladder, a global innovation curve, or causal effects of broad labels such as “Dark Ages.” Clinical superiority requires its own appropriate evidence; the current corpus has no performance measurements. No fully autonomous AI historian, complete world history or reconstructed physical genealogy is promised.

## Minimum scientific package

1. A frozen question, sampling frame and novelty review.
2. Independently validated measurement and acquisition processes.
3. One estimable historical contrast and a serious alternative explanation.
4. A replication or clearly bounded failure to generalize.
5. Permitted data, code, source locators and uncertainty sufficient for another team to audit the inference.

The atlas is the inspection interface for this package. The potential PNAS contribution is the tested explanation of technological persistence/change and knowledge movement. If only the infrastructure is achieved, the publication claim must be narrowed accordingly.

The 7 October [team review](research_cycles/2026-10-07/reviewer.md) located direct prior work on surgical tacit knowledge, manufacturing skill and trade catalogs. Generic knowledge-bundle claims do not establish novelty; the explicit cross-task syringe statements motivate a narrower prospective study.

## Implemented next stage: bounded documentary relations

The [Weiss 1863 study](research_cycles/2026-10-07-stage2/README.md) now contributes a complete 45-page screen, exact anchored nominations and scans, a reconciled eligibility register with controls, six repeated-assertion groups, and three matched target entries. Its 119 explicit relations are documentary mentions among 187 nominations; they are not 119 design-reuse events. Named entries remain unenumerated and independent human validation remains zero.

The empirical lead for C2 is now narrower: distinguish an identifiable retained functional core and changed task-specific working end from a seller's descriptive analogy. This is a candidate component-level test, not a demonstrated knowledge boundary. Source selection is directly visible in the maker's limitation on deformity apparatus at p. 43. C3 temporal contrasts and C4 historical-transfer attribution remain untested. No novelty claim is attached to using AI or to the general existence of modular tools.
