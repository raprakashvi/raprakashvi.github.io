How a catalogue record gets its domain

Every record of the three complete catalogues is in the same collection, with the same filters and the same views. The domain chips also give each domain a personality: an accent colour, one framing line and the default order of the collection, over the same pages. A record is placed as follows (scripts/web/unified/catalogue.py, full text in the rule):

  1. Flat first

    A print, drawing, photograph, letter, book, coin or map is never made surgical by a word in its title. A print about a surgeon is a print.

  2. Surgical

    The museum’s own words name a medical or surgical instrument: a Boerhaave classification under 10 Geneeskunde, hygiene, farmacie (packaging excluded), a NEMO medical-technology category, or a term from a stated word list in the name or categories.

  3. Science

    The T-030 codebook labelled the record with a named principle of physics, chemistry, life science or earth science.

  4. Not placed

    Everything else. The record stays in the collection, is counted on the Data page, and is never dropped.
